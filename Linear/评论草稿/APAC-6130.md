**前端需要跟着改**

前端是按原型做的（[APACFE-4659](https://linear.app/kira-learning/issue/APACFE-4659)、[APACFE-4890](https://linear.app/kira-learning/issue/APACFE-4890)）。下面这些原型和 PRD 不一致，稿已按 PRD 改：
- 顶栏返回：“Assignment Monitor” → “← Back to Assignment”。
- 翻页：“Previous Student” / “Next Student” → “← Prev” / “Next →”，位置不变。
- 教师标注：Read it / Needs support / Unclear → Read it / Still needs support / Can't tell（§6.2）。
- No usable recording 的提示 → “The recording has nothing to listen to.”。
- K 年级右栏：标题 → “Oral Retelling”；小字 → “Kindergarten's independent evidence is this retelling.”。
- 空态正文结尾：“…once they finish reading.” → “…once they finish.”。
- Behavior alert 摘要 → PRD 原句 “Here is a summary of the specific details of the student behavior alert.”。
- Open Response 标题：“Open response” → “Open Response”。
- 长回答：只放前 500 个字符，后面接 Show more。

PRD 没写、沿用原型的：各卡副标题、“Your mark · optional, after listening”、行为标签旁的时长、“They didn't tap any words.”、“No words tapped during Guided Reading.”、“Not scored” badge、Chat Activity 行里的学生姓名、播放器倍速。
